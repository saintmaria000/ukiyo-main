The word Ukiyo originally meant the “sorrowful world” — a world of hardship and impermanence. Over time its meaning was reversed. People began to embrace the fleeting nature of life, transforming the word into the idea of the “floating world.” Ukiyo Film was born from this idea. A creative studio that seeks to bring unreal ideas into reality — capturing this fragile yet beautiful world as joyful like the floating world, and beautiful even as the sorrowful world it once was.
